# Communication artifact catalog

The portfolio uses the same artifact breadth established in `neurocomms` and
`neuro-divergent`, while keeping the research evidence in its own repositories.

## Artifact families

- **Mobile apps:** mobile-ready communication and accessibility experiences,
  including the existing `neurocomms/my-visit-notes-mobile` work.
- **HTML demos:** static, reviewable research and product walkthroughs that can be
  opened without a backend or account.
- **Presentations:** slide, presenter, handout, live, and design views for the
  flagship research and applied projects.
- **PDFs:** printable reports, handouts, figures, and application attachments.
- **Video:** short overview, research walkthrough, product demo, and presentation
  rehearsal formats.
- **Style:** copy the established PhraseBench landing pattern—system typography,
  pale paper background, green action color, rounded white cards, compact eyebrow
  labels, and an explicit scope/limitations note.
