04 · INTEGRITY

How to read the evidence

  • Three-mouse result is labeled as three-mouse.
  • Descriptive within-animal bootstrap intervals are reported.
  • EEG negative result is retained.
  • Population-level uncertainty beyond three animals remains future work.
