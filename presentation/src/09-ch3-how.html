Chapter 3 · Measure · EEG study

Can sensors on the head tell when someone’s eyes are closed?

EEG is a cap of small sensors that pick up the brain’s electrical activity through the scalp. Closing your eyes is one of the clearest changes it can see.

  • The recording

    One person, two minutes

    14 sensors, about 128 readings a second, each moment marked open or closed.

  • Learn, then test

    Earlier, then later

    Learn from the first part, test on what comes after, like a real app would.

  • The score

    0.5 is a coin flip

    1.0 is always right. Below 0.5 is worse than guessing.
