What we found

In three sentences.

  1. 1 · The mouse study

    In mice, brain cells reacted differently to the very same picture depending on how alert the mouse was.

    Adding how alert each mouse was made our predictions of its brain cells 25–33% better, in all three mice.

  2. 2 · The brain-wave study

    A brain-wave headset on a person could not reliably tell even whether their eyes were open or closed.

    A computer spotted closed eyes for one stretch, then within two minutes was no better than a coin flip.

  3. 3 · The app

    So a helpful app should not try to read your brain: it should ask how you feel and show one small step at a time.

    Next Step asks “How much energy do you have today?” and on a low day shows only the next step.
