Chapter 5 · Test, then go further

Where this goes next.

  1. Now · done

    Two studies, one follow-up, two apps.

    The mouse and EEG studies, every result checked and rerunnable, plus experiment 2 on brain-wave bands. Next Step and Helper Jobs working, with the four design rules built in.

    Shown: on this site, in the slides and in the phone tour.

  2. Next · with an ASDRP mentor

    Run the three remaining data experiments.

    Drift in the EEG, using the data already here. Then timing (code already written) and groups of brain cells in the full mouse recordings.

    Aim: a first paper, on why the EEG drifts and what brain-wave bands can and cannot fix, for a student research journal such as the Journal of Emerging Investigators, plus a poster.

  3. Then · with people

    Study the design with the people it is for.

    Co-design sessions with Pragnya volunteers and families, then a usability test of the four design rules. Consent first, and tasks written down before anyone takes part.

    Aim: a second paper, on the design rules, and better apps.

  4. Longer term · the joined-up question

    Does designing for state help people learn?

    Compare the energy-aware design with a fixed one, for the same person over several weeks. Then ask whether the brain findings predict who it helps most.

    Aim: one research direction, where neuroscience and human–computer interaction meet.
