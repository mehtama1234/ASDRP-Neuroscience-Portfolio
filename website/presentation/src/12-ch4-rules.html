Chapter 4 · Design · Human–computer interaction

From findings to features.

  1. From chapter 2

    Show less when energy is low.

    Found: the same picture lands differently depending on state.

    In the apps: “How much energy do you have today?” On “A little”, just the next step.

  2. From chapter 3

    Ask, don’t guess.

    Found: a sensor read state once, then drifted, even with brain-wave bands.

    In the apps: the person says how they are. Nothing happens until they tap.

  3. From chapter 3

    Expect change halfway.

    Found: within two minutes the pattern shifted.

    In the apps: Save & pause, a note of where you stopped, break reminders.

  4. From real life

    When stuck, make it smaller.

    Seen: a task too big all at once is where people stop.

    In the apps: “I’m stuck” makes the step smaller and keeps your place.
