Chapter 3 · The result

On one stretch, yes. Over time, no.

On the last part of the recording the model scored 0.625 (0.728 once four broken readings were capped). Across five stretches it averaged 0.478: about a coin flip.

How to read the score

Where the results sit between guessing and always right.

Near 0.5 is no better than flipping a coin. Below 0.5 means the pattern it learned has flipped.

The score is called AUC.
