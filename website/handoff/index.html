Your four steps

Start with the first one. It changes the most.

  1. 1 · About 15 minutes

    Your real moment, and your parts.

    One real time from your own life when the same instruction worked once and was lost later, plus what you did in each app.

    Opens: the website, slides, video and Essay 1. Write your answers →

  2. 2 · About 30 minutes each

    Make both essays yours.

    Drafts that follow the same five chapters. Fill the parts in [brackets] and change anything that does not sound like you.

    Limit: 375 words each. Essay 1 → Essay 5 →

  3. 3 · About 10 minutes

    Check who did what.

    Eleven parts of the work. Confirm each one, or say what to change.

    Rule: only claim what you did and can explain. Check the list →

  4. 4 · Optional · About 20 minutes

    Your voice on the video.

    Read 16 short parts, one per slide, on a phone. Any you skip keep the computer voice.

    Then: send the recordings. Recording script →
