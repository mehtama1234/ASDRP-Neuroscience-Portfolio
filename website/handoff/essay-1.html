Essay 1 · draft

Why are you applying?

Chapter 1 · Real life

Your part Two or three sentences: one real time from your own life when the same instruction worked once and was lost a little later. What happened, and what you noticed. Share only what you are comfortable with.

The words were the same. Something inside me had changed. I wanted to know whether that was the brain itself: does what a brain is doing change what it takes in?

Chapter 2 · Understand

I tested it first in public recordings from mice watching pictures. I built two simple models of how thousands of brain cells responded. One knew only the picture. The other also knew how wide the mouse’s pupils were and how fast it was running. Knowing the mouse’s state made the guesses 25 to 33 percent better, in all three mice. When I gave the model the state from the wrong moments, the gain disappeared. State is not noise. It shapes what gets in.

Chapter 3 · Measure

Then I asked whether state can be read from outside the head. In a public EEG recording, a simple model could tell when someone’s eyes were closed on one stretch, but over two minutes it drifted to a coin flip. Your part Only if you ran or reran it yourself: I wrote down a follow-up plan before running it, using brain-wave bands, and it still did not hold up. I kept that result, because it changed what I believe.

Chapter 4 · Design

That result changed what I build. Next Step, my app for students, does not try to guess how someone is doing. It asks: how much energy do you have today? On a low day it shows just the next step, and it makes pausing and coming back easy.

Chapter 5 · What’s next

At ASDRP I want to do this properly, with a mentor: run the next experiments, write up why the EEG signal drifts, and test whether designs like mine actually help the people they are for. I am applying because I have started, and I want to learn to do it well.
