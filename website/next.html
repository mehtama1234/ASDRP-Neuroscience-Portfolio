What comes next

Five experiments, each from question to explanation.

Each gets an analysis written down before it runs, a held-out test, a control that could break it, and an honest write-up.

The five experiments

  • 1 · Planned

    Mouse behaviour timing

    Does behaviour from the same trial predict activity better than behaviour shifted to neighbouring trials? Needs the raw Sensorium recordings, which are not in this repository.

  • 2 · Ready to run

    EEG frequency bands

    Do brain-wave bands, like alpha that rises when eyes close, predict eye state better than raw signals over time? Uses the EEG file already here.

  • 3 · Planned

    Mouse neuron groups

    Is the gain spread across neurons, or concentrated in groups chosen in advance by depth and position? Needs raw responses and neuron positions.

  • 4 · Started

    EEG drift over time

    How stable is prediction across the recording, and does adjusting for drift help? Block-by-block scores and the glitch check are done.

  • 5 · Planned

    Usability with people

    Do the step-by-step designs make these research pages easier to follow? Needs consent, a task list written in advance and no unneeded personal data.

The rule we keep: a plan stays labelled as a plan until it has code, saved results, a rerun record, its limits written down and a page like the two studies.
